About Us


We convey our sincere gratitude and also take pleasure to introduce ourselves as a leading Software Developers. We have developed CHECKIN software for Hospitality Industry. We are having a team of professionals, which have deep experience of Software Development as well as the Hospitality Industry.

CheckIn is an integrated and comprehensive Hospitality system software solution consisting of several modules including Front Office, F & B, Back Office, Telephone interface, Spa, Swimming pool and Inventory Management. The software can be used in Hotel/Restaurant/Bar/Coffee Shop as a Stand-Alone or on a Multi User Network Environment and is user friendly that can be operated by a person with minimum computer qualifications. It generates a number of useful reports for the management in order to keep an eye on the sales of different saleable outlets of hotel. A number of security levels is yet another feature that allows you to avoid misuse of the software by the staff. In brief, Check-In is an eye that keeps you inform about day-to-day working of your hotel while you are out also.

The Software is operational many places at U.K./CHINA/Delhi/Rajasthan/Haryana/U.P./M.P. & Assam in many hotels, restaurant, bar. Even it is operational at many hotel management institute and Govt. operational hotels also.
